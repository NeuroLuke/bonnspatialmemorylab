Ann-Katrin, Louis, and Lukas attended the general assembly of NeurotechEU in Nijmegen! This three-day event brought together leading researchers, students, innovators, and NeurotechEU members from across Europe and Canada. It focused on the theme "Connected Brains, Connected Europe," highlighting how collaboration across disciplines and borders drives educational advancement and scientific progress.
